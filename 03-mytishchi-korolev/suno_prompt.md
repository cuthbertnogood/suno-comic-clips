Источник: метаданные трека Suno https://suno.com/song/f14a1480-c84d-4e58-970b-5c00cf9e02c8 (comic_inputs/02_mytishchi.json), модель v5.5.

## Title

Мытищи Королёв

## Style

Dark synth-pop with marching half-time drums and tense syncopated bass; verse rides sparse percussion and clipped vocal phrasing, pre-chorus narrows to pulsing pads and a filtered riser, chorus opens with stacked doubles and a chantable hook. Use short delay throws on place names, whispered ad-libs in transitions, and a sharp impact into each hook. Bright but urgent mix, close-mic and cinematic.
